### Portrait commission
- **Painter:** Orest Kiprensky ("The Prodigy": hiring him signals talent over birth; outcome less predictable; on success he becomes painter for life).
- **Type:** none (from features alone).
- **Head:** oblong face, high forehead, high cheekbones, hollow cheeks, tapered jaw, pointed chin, muscular neck.
- **Skin:** boyish, looks younger than his years; rosy northern complexion, faint rose colour, clear skin; **duelling scar on the cheek**.
- **Eyes:** ice blue, deep-set, even set, clear alert lids, long dark lashes, gaze straight at the viewer.
  Brows (TEXT WINS over the "fine, arched" chip): *low, set close to the eyes, angular "aquiline" brows, almost always frowning,
  giving the gaze a predatory intensity and beauty. The eyes are the most striking feature.* Face overall charming.
- **Nose & mouth:** straight Greek nose, fine, pointed tip; fine well-cut lips, level corners, a hint of a smile; ordinary ears.
- **Hair:** chestnut, wavy, *coup de vent* (unpowdered, against Paul's regulation), full hairline, no side-whiskers, clean-shaven.
- **Soul:** imperious; three-quarter right; chin raised; leaning forward, eager.
- **Dress:** Paul's Prussian-cut Preobrazhensky coat; orders: St Andrew sash & star, Maltese cross of St John, St Anna,
  star of St Alexander Nevsky; hand on sword hilt; half-length; stormy sky; dramatic side light.
- **Political reading:** the loyal son's coat with a rebel's hair. Paul's uniform and Paul's Maltese cross, but unpowdered windswept hair.

### Ten answers (verbatim)
1. **The sentry's glimpse:** "My silhouette. It is monstrous. 2 meters high and with very broad shoulders."
2. **Grandmother's verdict:** "European Princesses will fight for him."
3. **Father's mockery:** "My restless, eager posture, and elegant grace. I have done nothing about that."
4. **The hands:** "Large, muscular, but elegant hands."
5. **What the painter must leave out:** "I will not ask painter to omit anything, because i am sure in my face and its power. Everything horrible hides beneath it, in my lungs, chest, and skull."
6. **The forbidden wardrobe:** "I do not obey the ban and openly wear frock coats, laced shoes and waistcoats."
7. **The horse:** "White horse Appolon. I liked his grace, beauty, and chivalry." (white horse **Apollon**)
8. **Two kinds of whisper:** "Ladies of the court are openly delighted with me and comparing me to Apollo himself. Guards officers say that God given me a soldier body over a poet bearing."
9. **The morning:** "I wash with ice water, eat spartan food, and i shave myself."
10. **The mirror tonight:** "I am frightened by the fact that my death is not seen in my face; my chest is killing me, my lungs betray me, my head aches, i never sleep, and yet, my face is snow white and beautiful as always. But, maybe, that means i have hope?"
